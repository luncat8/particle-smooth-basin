# 0.4.3 heat-source lift restoration and preset balance worklog

## Review of 0.4.2

Comparing `6357fd1` (0.4.2) against `2d922f4` (0.4.1) and `e5b6c18` (0.4.0) exposed two
bugs that made lift forces look weak or dead across the presets:

1. **Heat-map double-attenuation and plume cooling (`js/engine.js`)**: in 0.4.1, every
   bead in the hot band relaxed toward `1.0` at rate `hotRate * u²(3-2u)`. In 0.4.2,
   `bandTarget` placed `u²(3-2u)` into the *cell target* `0.5 + 0.5*u²(3-2u)` and
   `exchangeHeat` *also* weighted the relaxation rate by `|ambient - 0.5| * 2` while
   relaxing `temp` toward `ambient`. That squared the heating drive (`w(u)²`), capped
   floor beads around `0.85` (since the tank floor sits at `0.935 * H`), and caused the
   upper half of the hot band (`ambient ~ 0.55–0.70`) to actively cool rising `0.85`
   beads back down at `hotRate`. Because upward lift in the presets comes from `T - 0.5`
   (both `thermalLift` density thinning and `sizeResponse * archSize` radius scaling),
   20 s seeded overturning (`up/down` across mid-bed) collapsed across all scenes:
   - `thermal`: `20.1/0.0 /s` (0.4.1) → `0.0/0.0 /s` (0.4.2)
   - `heat`: `36.0/5.8 /s` (0.4.1) → `4.2/0.0 /s` (0.4.2)
   - `thermalGrow`: `16.3/17.3 /s` (0.4.1) → `0.0/0.0 /s` (0.4.2)
   - `balanced`: `8.3/10.2 /s` (0.4.1) → `0.0/4.9 /s` (0.4.2)
   - `sparseRaft`: `7.8/2.2 /s` (0.4.0) → `1.8/0.0 /s` (0.4.2)
2. **Live coupling presets lacked buoyancy retuning (`js/engine.js`)**: the six 0.4.0
   couplings were tuned for `buoyancy = 1`, whereas the shipped `thermal` scene runs
   `buoyancy = 0.2` (calibrated for `arch = -10`). Changing only `size` and `arch` left
   `buoyancy = 0.2`, sinking the entire bed to `~480 px` with `0` overturning on all six
   couplings and `0` links on four of them.

Also caught during review: `solveHeatMap` recomputed `heatTarget` inside every
Gauss-Seidel sweep instead of once per solve; `index.html` / `RANGES` had dropped the `⇑`
prefix on the three force controls and widened the `arch` slider UI to `[-10, 10]`
instead of centering on the presets `[-10, 4]`; `experiments/balance.js` only ran
`shipped` instead of sweeping `PRESETS`; and `experiments/thermal-zone.js` did not
re-seed per candidate or measure bed-relative top/bottom quarters.

## Changes

- Treated `world.heat.field` (`0..1`, neutral at `0.5`) as signed coupling strength
  `signed = (field - 0.5) * 2`: cold cells (`signed < 0`) cool warmer beads toward the
  cold bath floor `clamp(0.32 - 0.30*strength, 0.02, 0.20)` with `bandTarget = 0.30*u`
  (matching 0.4.1's `1 - 0.6*u` cold weight), neutral cells (`0.5`) leave beads to
  conduction, and hot cells (`signed > 0`) heat beads toward `1` at rate
  `hotRate * signed`.
- Preallocated `world.heat.target` in `initHeatMap` and filled cell targets once per
  `solveHeatMap` call before iterating.
- Unified `PRESETS` so every entry carries `settings`: the six `coupling` presets now set
  `{ size, arch, buoyancy }` (`0.9` for `shrinkFlow`, `0.6` for `bigFloats`, `0.55` for
  the `±4` convection and stable pairs), working on any live bed without re-dealing.
- Restored `⇑` on the three force controls and tightened `RANGES.arch.ui` to `[-10, 4]`
  (world clamp stays `[-10, 10]`).
- Updated `tests.html` (`checkThermalZone` now measures bed-relative quarters, uses the
  top-quarter median so breaching plumes do not masquerade as a warm crust, and requires
  `bottom > 0.80`), added preset overturning assertions to `experiments/settings.js`,
  expanded `experiments/balance.js` across all 11 presets, and re-seeded
  `experiments/thermal-zone.js` per candidate. Bumped `VERSION` to `0.4.3`.
