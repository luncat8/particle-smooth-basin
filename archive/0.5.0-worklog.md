# 0.5.0 beads feed the heat map worklog

## Review of 0.4.3

- **Zone labels were keyed to the live field version.** `js/render.js` rebuilt
  `COLD BAND END · …` / `HOT BAND START · …` whenever `world.heat.version` changed.
  That was free while the field changed only on a band move; a two-way field bumps the
  version every frame, so the labels would have concatenated two strings per frame in
  the render loop. The field now carries `bandVersion` (bumped only by a band solve) and
  the labels follow that.
- **`checkThermalZone` dealt an unseeded bed.** Three seeds sampled from the code's own
  RNG: `0x9e3779b9` lay the bed top at 247 px, `0x2545f491` at 257 px, `0x51ed270b` at
  393 px — the last one never reaches the cold band, so the check fails on a scene
  outcome rather than a regression (it also fails that way at `feed = 0`, so 0.4.3 shipped
  with the same lottery). The check is now dealt from `0x9e3779b9` through a
  `withSeed(seed, body)` helper, so the log is comparable across versions.
- **`experiments/harness.js` hard-coded the check count** (`checks.length === 8`) and two
  `RESULT … checks 8/8` regexes; both moved to nine.
- The `feed` write is read back through `heatAt`, a bilinear read of four cells. The first
  version of the browser check sampled a fixed pixel while the bead drifted 0.4 px across
  a row edge, so the write landed in the neighbouring cell and the check read a quarter of
  it (0.538 from 0.500). Both are recorded in `findings-pitfalls-skills.md`.

## The model

Direction A of `0.5-draft.md`: the bands stay the reservoir, beads add transport.

- **Write.** Once per rendered frame (not per substep) each bead adds its temperature to
  the cell it covers, area-weighted: `bead[c] = Σ T·πr²/A_cell`, `cover[c] = Σ πr²/A_cell`.
- **Blend.** `m = min(1, cover)`, `target = clamp(band + feed·m·(Tbead − band), 0, 1)`.
  The cell is blended *toward the bead*, not toward "hot": a bead that already sits at its
  cell's temperature writes nothing.
- **Solve.** The bands still settle with up to 240 Gauss-Seidel sweeps when their geometry
  changes; while the feed is on, six sweeps per frame track the moving targets, so a wake
  trails a bead by a few frames.
- **Off switch.** `feed = 0` sets the targets to the band profile, skips the per-frame
  update entirely, and re-solves on the way down, so the field is the 0.4.3 map and the
  wakes are gone rather than frozen into the picture.

The first attempt wrote the raw temperature (`target = band + feed·(T − 0.5)`). Measured at
20 s on the shipped scene it *amplified*: mean tank temperature 0.79 → 0.87 at `feed 0.5`,
0.96 at 0.75 and 1.00 at 1 (single-digit cold beads, no bonds). The reason is that the
field is a source strength, not a temperature: a warmer cell drives its beads further
toward 1, so a dense hot bed heats its own water and then itself. Blending toward the bead
keeps the loop bounded, because the bead's own contribution vanishes at equilibrium.

## Changes

- `js/engine.js`: `heat.band` (band profile), `heat.bead`/`heat.cover` (area-weighted
  write), `heat.target` (blend), `bandVersion`; `fillHeatBands`, `splatBeadHeat`,
  `fillHeatTargets`, `relaxHeat`, `solveHeatMap`, `updateHeatField`; `HEAT_LIVE_SWEEPS = 6`;
  `heatFeed` on the world, `RANGES.feed`, `setHeatFeed`, the `feed` settings key, and
  `updateHeatField` at the end of `stepWorld`.
- Scene presets carry their own feed: `thermal 0.20`, `heat 0.05`, `thermalGrow 0.10`,
  `balanced 0.10`, `sparseRaft 0.15`; live couplings leave it alone.
- `js/app.js` / `index.html`: a **Heat feed** row after conduction, with the readout and
  the boot sync coming from the engine's `CONTROLS`/`RANGES` tables as every other slider.
- `js/render.js`: zone labels follow `bandVersion`; the wash needed no change — it is
  already painted from the solver's grid.
- `experiments/heat-feed.js` (new, in `run_tests.py`): every scene at its shipped feed next
  to the bands alone, with drift, crust, field range, clamp share and per-frame cost; a
  feed list and a seed argument probe the transition by hand
  (`node experiments/heat-feed.js 20 0,0.5,0.75,1`).
- `experiments/thermal.js`: feed default and clamp, one parked bead lifting/lowering its
  own cell, `feed 0` leaving the field frozen, the feed going to 0 clearing the wake, and
  the field staying inside `0..1` through a splash at full feed.
- `experiments/settings.js`: couplings leave `feed` alone, a paste of `9` clamps to `1`.
- `experiments/smoke.js`: the slider is synced from the engine on boot, drives the world
  through `input`, appears in the copied line, and Defaults restores it.
- `tests.html`: a `heat-feed` check (cell read, `feed 0` frozen, hot up, cold down, field
  bounded after a splash) and the seeded `thermal-zone` bed; nine checks.
- `VERSION` 0.5.0; README controls, shipped-scene numbers and the check list.

The shipped scene keeps its character to ~0.45 and loses its crust between 0.5 and 0.75;
`sparseRaft` tolerates the full range. `heat` is the sensitive one (its cold/hot sides are
already near balance at `feed 0`: top/bottom quarter 0.61/0.66): at `0.05` the crust goes
99 → 154 cold beads and 85 → 354 links, at `0.20` the mean falls 0.63 → 0.49. Hence its
preset feeds 0.05, and the conservative defaults everywhere else.

Guard the harness runs (`node experiments/heat-feed.js`, 15 s, five scenes): worst drift
0.031–0.044, crust keeps 82–96 % of its cold beads, worst cost +0.07–0.11 ms/frame, field
inside `0..1` through a splash, `feed 0` bit-identical to the band solve.

`python3 run_tests.py --node-only` — all seven node checks pass (`smoke`, `thermal`,
`bench`, `thermal-zone`, `heat-feed`, `settings`, `harness`), log
`experiments/logs/0.5.0-tests-*.log`. The harness lint runs all nine browser checks in the
sandbox; the seeded shipped-scene check reads 147/648 cold, 65 bonds, top/bottom 0.33/0.98,
bed top 254 px (0.4.3, unseeded: 146 cold, 36 bonds, 0.37/0.96, 248 px).

`node experiments/balance.js 20` (seeded, 20 s) with the new presets: `thermal`
21.3→27.8 hot beads/s overturning and 116→301 links, `heat` 353 links and 154 cold at
feed 0.05, `thermalGrow` 1078 links, `balanced` 924 links and 19.4/23.4 overturning,
`sparseRaft` 503 links. Per-frame cost 0.31–1.19 ms. The long-run flotation of the dense
beds (a fifth of `thermal` above the waterline by 20 s) is unchanged by the feed and
pre-dates this version.

Not validated here: canvas pixels and real frame times — this sandbox has no browser. The
browser matrix now runs the shipped feed on every row, so `heat cap` and `stirred-cap` in
the next capture are the budget evidence for the per-frame splat and six sweeps.
