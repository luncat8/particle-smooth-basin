# 0.4.0 scene balance and tunable settings worklog

## the complaint, measured

The 0.3.3 default was a full glass of beads: 810 at 50% coverage, waterline at 42% of the height, a bed that reached 297 px above the waterline and stopped well short of the cold band. Bonds only existed because the band had been pushed down to 0.40 to meet the pile. The scene asked for was the opposite — a sparse bed, water over it, the bed's top inside the cold band, and visible overturning.

`experiments/balance.js` (new) made the scene measurable: full settings objects in, geometry and behaviour out. Two bugs surfaced immediately.

- **The spawn lattice was tighter than the beads.** `sx = 2.4 * rBar` cannot hold beads of up to `1.28 * rBar` radius, and `sy = 1.87 * rBar` overlapped every row, so the solver spent the first second pushing the bed out of itself. Between spawn and 2 s the bed's top moved 200–350 px upward; on the way it threw single beads at the ceiling, which the first version of "bed top" metric reported as the bed's surface. `spawnBed` now packs rows from the radius it actually places, and the metric is a 2% quantile.
- **Size coupling does not convect.** `arch-only`, `flat`, `size-only` and `shrink` variants all sat at `flux ≈ 0` over 16–20 s: hot beads shrank, neighbours moved a little, nothing overturned. The mechanism that works is an effective density `dens * (1 - 2*lift*(T - 0.5))` (`world.thermalLift`, the **Heat lifts** slider), plus a hot band thick enough to touch the bed — `hotStart` 0.84 → 4.0 hot beads rising per second, 0.80 → 7.3.

## what shipped

- `js/engine.js`: `spawnBed` packs by radius; `THERMAL_LIFT = 0.40` with the density term in `substep`; `COLD_ZONE_END = 0.56`, `HOT_ZONE_START = 0.78`, `WATER_FRAC = 0.30`, `DEFAULT_FILL = 0.21`, `DEFAULT_SIZE = -0.35`; `setFillFraction`'s minimum lowered from 0.3 to 0.08 (the old floor silently overrode every sparse fill); `setThermalLift`; and the settings layer `SETTINGS` / `readSettings` / `applySettings`.
- `index.html`: eight new sliders (cold depth, hot top, cold power, hot power, heat lifts, bond strength, bond vs temperature, water level) plus Copy / Paste / Defaults and an echo line.
- `js/app.js`: `syncPanel()` drives the whole panel from the world (one table, no per-slider duplication), `applyAndDeal()` for paste and defaults, `copySettings()` with an `execCommand` fallback, `pasteSettings()` with a prompt and a JSON error path, and `percentValue` fixed to stop double-scaling the percentage sliders.
- `experiments/settings.js` (new node check), `experiments/smoke.js` extended to boot from deliberately wrong markup and prove the panel re-syncs, `tests.html`'s `thermal-zone` check rewritten for the shipped scene (8 s, waterline and cold-band geometry, few beads above the surface), and the perf matrix now starts with a `shipped` row in both quick and full runs.
- `experiments/bench.js`: `singleBead` neutralizes the size/Archimedes/lift couplings, which a live size coupling had been quietly reshaping (`baseSize` 0 → r collapsed).

## validation

- `python3 run_tests.py --node-only` — pass, six node checks: smoke, thermal, bench (0.30 ms/frame at n=356, near-cap 1500 2.10 ms/frame, pairs 3683, equilibrium 0.500/1.000), thermal-zone (shipped row 365 avg / 451 max links, 205 cold), settings, harness (8 checks; `thermal-zone` reports `cold 205/340 · 457 bonds · top 0.12 bottom 0.95 · 0 above water · bed top 241 px`).
- `node experiments/balance.js 40` — shipped scene: 340 beads, 484 links, 223 cold, top/bottom 0.15/0.95, 5.5 hot beads rising per second, 1/340 above the waterline, 0.34 ms/frame.
- Numbers are in `experiments/logs/0.4.0.txt`; the plan and the rejected candidates are in `0.4-plan.md`.
- Not validated here: how it looks, and real frame times. This sandbox has no browser, so the matrix and the pixels still need one capture from a real machine.
