# 0.3.3 cold band reaches the bed worklog

## review of 0.3.2

The capture `0.3.2-tests-2026-10-26-00-42` (Windows, Chrome 151, DPR 1.25, NVIDIA) confirmed the three fixes and re-ran the matrix: `timer 5.0 us` instead of `0.00`, the versioned log name, one browser section per run, and 11/11 cases inside the 16.7 ms budget with the worst frame p95 at 2.09 ms. Frame cost is unchanged from the previous capture within noise, so nothing regressed. With the frame budget settled by two independent runs, the plan's remaining item — visible thermal bonds at the default settings — was the next step.

## the problem, measured

`-0.3-plan.md` asked for cold links that are visible without extreme splashes, and the 0.24 cold band never delivered that. The physics band and the drawn gradient were two hard-coded `0.24`/`0.76` pairs in different files, so the band could not be tuned or measured without editing both. `experiments/thermal-zone.js` now sweeps the pair against a seeded bed:

- at `0.24`, after 8 s at the default 1× source, only 23 of 810 beads are cold enough to bond and the bed carries 60 shallow links; the band ends above the settled bed, at the waterline.
- every candidate from `0.36` up costs the same frame time within noise (0.78–0.87 ms/frame seeded), so the choice is about look, not budget.

## implementation

- `world.coldZoneEnd` / `world.hotZoneStart` hold the bands as shares of H, defaulting to `COLD_ZONE_END = 0.40` and `HOT_ZONE_START = 0.76`; `exchangeHeat` reads them and `render.js` draws its two gradients from the same numbers, so the visible band is the measured band.
- The cold band now ends at 40% of the view, which at 1280×800 is 320 px — inside the settled bed after the re-deal and permanently flooded by the 50% waterline. Result at the default rates: the bed's upper half is cold (top band 0.25, bottom band 0.83), ~500 active bonds, and the bottom stays fluid. A splash tears the crust (654 links half a second later) and it re-forms (758).
- `experiments/thermal-zone.js` (new) prints the geometry table, then the source-rate table at the shipped band, from the engine's own constants so the table cannot drift from the page. It is part of `run_tests.py`'s node phase, so every run log carries the comparison.
- `tests.html` gained a `thermal-zone` check (now eight checks): at the default rates, after 4 s, at least 100 of the beads must be bondable and at least 200 bonds active, the top band must be colder than 0.40 and the bottom warmer than 0.60, and with the source off no bond may form at all. The thresholds sit far from both candidates (≈500 links shipped, 60 at the old band) and hold across three seeds.
- The zone change survives the extremes it is exposed to: 60 s soak at defaults gives 846 links / mean T 0.59 / max|v| 3; at the 1500-bead cap 1365 links / mean T 0.63 / max|v| 16; `NaN=0` in both. A hot-band change was tested (`0.70`, `0.82`) and rejected: it raises the bed's mean temperature and the churn without improving the cold-bond count.

## validation

- `python3 run_tests.py --node-only` — pass, five node checks: smoke, thermal, bench (default 0.67 ms/frame, near-cap 1500 1.26 ms/frame, pairs 2209/3754, equilibrium 0.500/1.000), thermal-zone (tables above), harness lint (8 checks, `thermal-zone` reporting `cold 193/810 · 498 bonds · top 0.31 bottom 0.77 at 1x`).
- `node experiments/thermal.js` — pass, unchanged; the placed-pair temperature checks are unaffected because they place beads at y=18 and y=470 in a 480-tall world.
- Numbers are in `experiments/logs/0.3.3.txt`.
- Not validated here: how it looks. The bond count is the proxy; the visual judgement (does the crust read cold, does the thaw read) needs the browser, and the live preview runs `tests.html` and `index.html` from the same server.

## next step

Open `index.html` at the defaults and judge the band by eye: the bed's top half should look cold in the temperature view and hold visible links, the bottom should stay fluid, and `SPACE` should tear and re-form the crust. If it reads too subtle, move `COLD_ZONE_END` to `0.44` (606 links, same frame cost); if the bed looks over-gelled, back off to `0.36`. Either way run `python3 run_tests.py` and compare against `experiments/logs/0.3.3.txt` — the `thermal-zone` table and the `cold-links` perf row both move with the band.
