# 0.5.0 beads feed the heat map

## goal

The heat map is a pure source: the two bands set every cell, and the beads only
read it. A cold crust therefore chills nothing but itself, and a plume rises
through water that knows nothing about it. 0.5.0 makes the field two-way — every
bead writes its own temperature into the cell it occupies — so the map carries
transported heat: a hot wake behind a rising plume, a cold trail under a sinking
crust bead, a crust locally thinned where a plume punches through it.

Direction A of `0.5-draft.md`. The bands stay the reservoir; beads add transport
on top of them.

## the model

The field keeps its meaning: a signed source strength around a neutral `0.5`,
sampled by the solver and painted by the renderer. Two changes make it two-way.

1. **Bead write.** Once per rendered frame (not per substep — the field is a
   coarse, slow medium), each bead adds its signed temperature deviation to its
   cell, weighted by the share of the cell it covers:
   `bead[c] = Σ (T_i − 0.5) · πr_i²/A_cell`, `cover[c] = Σ πr_i²/A_cell`.
   A cell's transported deviation is `bead[c] / max(1, cover[c])`: a partly
   covered cell is scaled by how much bead area sits in it, a packed cell reports
   the bead mean instead of summing to unrealistic excursions.
2. **Blended target.** A cell's target is `clamp(band[c] + feed · deviation, 0, 1)`,
   where `band` is the band profile the solver already computes and `feed` is the
   one new control. `feed = 0` reproduces the 0.4.3 field exactly, bands only.

The write is the bead's temperature (`0.5` neutral), the same scale the band
profile uses; the *blend* is toward the bead, never toward "hot", so a bead that
already sits at its cell's temperature adds nothing. Writing the raw deviation from
neutral instead was measured and rejected: the field is a source strength, so a
warmer cell drives its beads further toward `1` rather than toward itself, and the
shipped scene went from a cold crust to a fully hot tank at `feed 0.75`.

**Solving.** The bands still settle with the full Gauss-Seidel solve (up to 240
sweeps) when their geometry changes. While beads feed the map, the field is warm
and the targets move at bead speed, so six sweeps per frame are enough to track
them; the wake trails a few frames behind the bead, which is what makes it read
as a wake. A band drag or a resize still triggers the full solve, so the bands
never look ragged mid-drag.

**Bounds.** `feed` is a slider (`0..1`), the target is clamped, the cold floor is
already clamped to `0.02..0.20` and the hot side to `1`, and the band target
still pulls every cell: the coupling cannot run away to NaN, only shift where
the tank sits between the cold and hot reservoirs. Drift is the thing to measure,
not to assume — see below.

**Known artifact.** A bead writes its own cell, so a lone bead nudges its own
source a little (standard particle-in-cell self-force). At the shipped feed this
is a fraction of the band term and it is bounded by the clamp; it is not worth a
per-bead exclusion buffer at this fidelity.

## controls and scenes

- **Heat feed** (`feed`, `0..1`, shipped `0.20`) joins the *Heat rates* group next
  to conduction; it is part of the settings line (`"feed":0.2`), so a copied scene
  carries how strongly its beads feed the map.
- Every scene preset names its own `feed`: `thermal 0.20`, `heat 0.05`,
  `thermalGrow 0.10`, `balanced 0.10`, `sparseRaft 0.15` — each the highest value
  that keeps that scene's own cold/hot balance at 20 s (the `heat` scene is the
  marginal one). The six live couplings keep whatever the scene had (they only retune
  size, Archimedes exponent and buoyancy).
- `setHeatFeed` re-solves the bands when the value reaches 0, so turning the feed
  off drops the wakes instead of freezing them into the picture.

## rendering

The wash is already painted from the solver's grid, so a live field shows up with
no draw-path change. The label cache (`COLD BAND END · …`) must key on the band
geometry, not the field version, or it would rebuild strings every frame now that
the field changes every frame; the renderer gets a separate band stamp.

## measurement

New `experiments/heat-feed.js`: the shipped scene and the sparse raft at
`feed = 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1`, 20 s seeded, reporting per candidate

- mean tank temperature at 5/10/20 s — the freeze/thaw drift that would show a
  feedback loop running away;
- cold beads, active links, top/bottom quarter temperature — the crust the scene
  is judged by;
- overturning (hot beads carried above the bed mid-line per second) — convection
  must not collapse;
- field statistics: mean |field − band|, the share of cells clamped at 0 or 1,
  and the share of cells carried more than 0.1 away from their band target;
- ms/frame, so the per-frame splat and six sweeps are accounted for.

The check asserts what would make the feature wrong rather than what makes it
pretty: `feed = 0` leaves the field bit-identical to the band solve; a hot bead
raises `heatAt` at its own cell and a cold one lowers it; the field stays inside
`0..1` and finite through a splash; and after 20 s the mean tank temperature at
every feed stays within `0.08` of the bands-only run.

## tests

- `experiments/thermal.js`: feed default and clamp, a lone hot/cold bead moving
  the field at its cell, feed 0 leaving the field untouched by beads, the field
  relaxing back to the bands when the feed returns to 0.
- `experiments/settings.js`: `feed` round-trips, clamps like every other key,
  scene presets carry it, couplings leave it alone, and a pasted feed of 1 stays
  finite for 10 s.
- `experiments/smoke.js`: the new row is synced from the engine on boot, drives
  the world through `input`, appears in the copied line, and the restored scene
  presets compare key for key.
- `tests.html`: a `heat-feed` check (lone hot bead against a feed-0 field) and the
  existing matrix, whose every row now runs the shipped feed, so the per-frame
  field cost is in the frame budget rather than in a special case.
- `run_tests.py` gains `experiments/heat-feed.js` in the node half.

## acceptance

- The default scene keeps its 0.4.3 character (crust, ~20 hot beads/s
  overturning, top/bottom contrast) with visible warmth in the plumes' wakes and
  a visibly thinned crust where a plume breaks through.
- `feed = 0` is a complete off switch: same field, same frame cost as 0.4.3.
- No drift: the 20 s mean tank temperature at the shipped feed is inside the
  bands-only bound, and the cold-bead count moves by less than a quarter.
- Frame cost at 1500 beads stays under the browser budget; the node throughput
  and the browser matrix both report it.
