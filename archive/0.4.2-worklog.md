# 0.4.2 heat-source map, restored presets and stabilization worklog

## Review of 0.4.1

The 0.4.1 build did the GUI and clipboard work, but four things were left in a state a
reviewer has to catch, and the task here was to fix them before adding anything:

- the cold/hot bands were still two hand-painted gradients in `render.js` whose numbers
  had to be kept in step with the solver by hand;
- every slider moved the preset select to "custom", including the sliders a preset had
  just set, because the app had no idea which values came from the preset;
- `experiments/bench.js` measured Archimedes equilibrium with the shipped buoyancy
  (0.2×) still in place, so a half-density bead read `frac = 1.000` and the printout
  said "want ~0.500" next to a number that failed;
- `experiments/thermal-zone.js` printed its source-rate label twice per row and its
  header claimed `conduction 1x` while the sweep actually ran the shipped 0.25×.

The first two were replaced by the heat map and the preset kinds below; the last two are
one-line fixes plus an assertion in the bench.

## Presets

The 0.4.1 preset list had been rewritten from scratch and the six 0.4.0 couplings were
gone. They are back at the end of the list, as couplings rather than scenes, so that
picking one works on whatever bed is on screen:

- scenes (re-deal the bed): `thermal` (the shipped default), `heat`, `thermalGrow`,
  `balanced`, and `sparseRaft` — the 0.4.0 default restored value for value;
- couplings (live bed): `shrinkFlow`, `bigFloats`, `shrinkConvection`,
  `growConvection`, `shrinkStable`, `growStable`, i.e. the six `[size, arch]` pairs
  from `e5b6c18`.

`PRESETS` carries `kind`, `applyPreset` switches on it, and the app builds the select
from the table with an optgroup per kind. A coupling on the 0.4.1 default produces no
active links — that is expected, the six pairs need a scene with real buoyancy, and
`sparseRaft` is the scene they were tuned against.

## Heat-source map

The two band gradients are replaced by one field. `world.heat` holds a `48 × n` grid
over the whole window; `bandTarget` is the 1-D profile the sliders name, `heatTarget`
fades it to the room's neutral through the glass, and `solveHeatMap` relaxes the grid
toward it with Gauss-Seidel, warm-started from the previous field. The solve runs once
per frame at most, only when a band moved: 6.15 ms cold, 0.41–3.99 ms warm (4–7 sweeps
to 5e-4 at `HEAT_PULL = 3`).

The renderer paints the same grid — blue cold, clear neutral, amber hot, alpha
proportional to `|t - 0.5|` — as a wash behind the water, and as the whole frame in the
new **heat map** view. Beads draw at 0.42 alpha in that view so the field reads through
them. The band labels and the dashed drag lines are drawn from the same numbers.

The important part of the model is not the solve but the rate: a bead relaxes toward the
field weighted by how far the field is from neutral. The first version relaxed every
bead toward its cell, and the clear middle of the map then acted as a thermostat,
re-warming the bed as fast as the cold band chilled it (300 px sat at 0.48, 19 of 648
beads cold, 9 links, against 130 cold in the 0.4.1 build). Weighting the rate restores
conduction as the thing that carries the cold into the bed: 133 cold of 648 and 19
links, with the top quarter at 0.39 and the floor at 0.69 — the 0.4.1 bed, sourced from
a smooth map instead of two hard stripes.

## Controls and settings

- `RANGES` is the single source for a control's world clamp, slider span and step; the
  markup carries ids only, and `configureControls` writes min/max/step from the table.
- The two band edges are draggable in the tank. `pickZone` only returns a line when no
  bead is within 16 px, which is also the radius the hover cursor tests, so the cursor
  always predicts what a click grabs. The waterline was tried as a third handle and
  removed: at the default scene it sits 9 px from the cold edge (0.30 vs 0.30 of the
  window) and could never win the pick.
- `SETTERS` maps every settings key to its setter, and `setSetting` is what the sliders,
  a paste and a preset all use. `view` is part of the settings JSON, unknown keys are
  returned to the caller and named in the panel echo, and the two bands stay at least
  `ZONE_GAP = 0.05` apart whichever one is set.
- The panel is grouped (bands / heat rates / convection forces / bonds / scene), the
  view button cycles density → temperature → heat map, and `1`/`2`/`3` select a view
  directly.

## Tests

- `experiments/smoke.js` was rewritten for the new page: boot from the engine's shipped
  scene (not from the markup's placeholder values), all fifteen sliders, the preset
  optgroups and both kinds, a band drag found by probing the page's own cursor, pointer
  cancel, DPR change, clipboard copy/paste/reset, and `?profile=1`.
- `experiments/settings.js` covers the JSON path: round trip, partial paste, unknown
  keys, clamping, live fields, the view key, every preset, heat-map geometry and 10 s of
  stability.
- `tests.html` gains a heat-map perf case (`heat cap`, near-cap bed in the heat view)
  and now switches the view through `setView` instead of the dead `world.temperatureView`
  flag, so the temperature and heat cases really measure those draw paths.
- A frame-budget verdict cannot be asserted in the sandbox: the page runs through
  `vm.runInContext`, where V8 cannot optimize it and the same scene costs ~10x. The page
  reads `?budget=` and the node harness asks for 60 ms; a browser run still reads 16.7.

## Validation

`python3 run_tests.py --node-only` passed all six checks at 0.4.2: smoke, thermal,
bench, thermal-zone, settings, harness. Shipped-scene numbers from the sandbox run:
648 beads, 136 cold enough to bond after 8 s, 16 active links, top quarter 0.40 against
0.69 at the floor, 2 beads above the waterline, bed top 246 px. Save:
`experiments/logs/0.4.2-tests-2026-10-26-06-05-36.log`.

Not validated: pixels and real-device frame times for the heat view. No browser is
available in the sandbox, so the browser half of the log is still the user's run.
