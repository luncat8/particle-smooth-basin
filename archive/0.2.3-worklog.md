# 0.2.3 hot-path review worklog

## review of 0.2.2

Common-case issues found in the shipped CPU/Canvas2D build:

- `pointer.active` stayed true after the first hover and was never cleared on pointer-up, so an idle cursor kept applying the radial stir brush and forced a pointer-force check for every bead every substep.
- Thermal links were append-only. Warm links stayed in the solver, render, and stats forever, and separated cold pairs could keep long-range distance constraints. `linkA` never grew even though `pairA` could, so a busy bed could silently stop accepting new bonds at 12000 entries.
- `waveStep` ran once per rendered frame instead of inside fixed physics substeps, making wave motion depend on display refresh rate.
- With conduction at zero, source-only temperature updates skipped the final clamp; a bead dragged above `y=0` could relax toward a negative cold target.
- DPR was sampled once at startup and static tank outlines were rebuilt every frame.
- The default `sizeResponse=0` still recomputed every radius and inverse mass every substep, and contact relaxation recomputed pair radius sums three times per substep.

## implementation

- Replaced `pointer.active` with `down` as the single force gate and clear pointer velocity on down/up. Pointer cancel no longer satisfies the short-click bead spawn, and DPR is refreshed on resize.
- Rebuilt cold bonds from the current broadphase each substep. Two open-addressed key tables preserve rest distances between substeps; warm or separated pairs thaw. All cold bonds remain in `linkN`, while a compacted `linkActiveN` prefix contains only mechanically effective constraints for solver, renderer, and stats. Stale stiffness slots are zeroed.
- Cached grid cell indices and summed pair radii from broadphase; pair and link buffers now grow together on exceptional overflow. Thermal-size and inverse-mass refresh is skipped when size response is zero and is done once when size/radius/mass controls change.
- Moved the wave field into the fixed substep loop at 60 Hz and clamped the cold-source field to a normalized target above the canvas.
- Cached static tank/rim `Path2D` objects and context text/line state on resize; render loops only effective links and hoists band bounds out of the link loop.
- Added seeded bench output for effective/cold link peaks and focused checks for persistent rest length, thawing, no long-range links, hover gating, normalized source-only temperature, and 60/120 Hz wave parity.
- Rewrote `0.3-plan.md` around measured end-to-end frame time and a WebGPU go/no-go gate. Updated `0.2-plan.md`, `README.md`, and `findings-pitfalls-skills.md` to describe the final link lifecycle and cached geometry.

## validation

- `node --check` on all page and experiment scripts — clean.
- `node experiments/thermal.js` — passed rates, sizing, buoyancy bounds, rest persistence, thawing, pointer gating, normalized temperature, fixed-step wave parity, and coverage.
- `node experiments/smoke.js` — passed classic-script boot/render with cached `Path2D` assets, six presets, controls, DPR change, and pointer cancel.
- `node experiments/bench.js` — three-run seeded median: default 810 beads at 0.56 ms/frame and near-cap 1500 beads at 1.27 ms/frame; no NaN at defaults, after splash, or at `archSize=10`, `sizeResponse=-1`, `buoyancy=1.5`; equilibrium 0.500/1.000 unchanged. Alternating same-seed A/B against the previous engine measured about 25% less default and 24% less near-cap simulation time.
- Stress-ran all six presets and a near-cap pointer/splash scene: no NaN, temperatures stayed in [0,1], and `linkActiveN <= linkN <= pairN <= capacity`.
- `git diff --check` — clean. Numbers in `experiments/logs/0.2.3.txt`.
