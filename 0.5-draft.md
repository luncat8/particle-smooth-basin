# 0.5 options

Written at the end of 0.4.2, where the thermal bands became one solved heat field that
the renderer paints, the solver samples, and the heat view shows. Everything below is an
option, not a decision: each entry says what it would cost and what would have to be
measured to call it done. The paintable source idea (formerly B) is deferred in
`deferred-plan.md`.

## A. A heat map beads can write into — shipped as 0.5.0

The field is currently a pure source: only the two bands set it. Beads could also push
their own temperature into the cells they occupy, so a cold crust chills the water it
sits in and a rising hot bead drags a warm wake behind it.

*Shipped*: `feed` blends each cell toward the bead mean it covers (`target = band +
feed·m·(T − band)`), six Gauss-Seidel sweeps per frame track it, the scenes ship their
own feed, and `0` is an exact off switch. The raw-temperature variant in the paragraph
below is what the measurements rejected: it amplifies instead of equalizing. Numbers in
`archive/0.5.0-worklog.md`.

- grid stays at 48 cells; adding a per-cell write pass is O(beads) per frame, no new
  allocations, and the solve already runs on the same grid;
- the danger is feedback: a cold bed would cool the whole tank and then bond harder.
  The band target can stay the anchor (`HEAT_PULL` already pulls cells back), so the
  drift is bounded by the pull;
- measure: mean tank temperature over 30 s at the default and at `sparseRaft`, plus the
  cold-bead/bond counts the 0.4.2 worklog records as the baseline. A scene that slowly
  freezes or thaws against `HEAT_PULL` would show up there first. (`experiments/heat-feed.js`
  does this per scene and is part of the node checks.)

## C. Settle and archive the renderer — 0.5.2 capture, 0.5.3 guide

The heat wash remains a 48-cell grid scaled with `drawImage`. The paired seeded cap
cases at DPR 1 and 2 have the same 1500-bead bed and 586 links; the user's 0.5.2
Windows/Chrome capture reports callback p95 2.55 / 2.60 ms and render p95 1.09 /
1.11 ms, with rAF p95 5.59 ms in both. The user reports the picture looks good.
`visual.html` now gives that visual judgment an explicit four-step checklist and a
separate saved review, because the canvas smoke check only proves bead repaint,
not wash smoothness. Keep the grid and draw path unless a later guided review reveals
banding; if it does, try a higher `HEAT_GRID` before a per-pixel draw path.

## D. WebGPU gate — remains closed after the 0.5.1 browser capture

The Windows/Chrome/NVIDIA capture in `experiments/logs/0.5.1-tests-2026-10-26-08-50-03.log`
measures the full CPU/Canvas2D matrix at 13/13 under 16.7 ms, worst p95 2.68 ms at
1500 beads and rAF p95 ~5.6 ms. The heat view at DPR 1 is 2.46 ms. The 0.5.2
paired DPR 2 heat case stays at 2.60 ms p95. There is no performance reason for a
WebGPU port; the gate is closed.
WebGPU remains an optional separate-port investigation, not a default renderer switch.

## E. Packaging the six couplings — shipped as 0.5.1

The six couplings retune size, Archimedes exponent and buoyancy on a live bed. An optional
**Couplings × scenes** run in `tests.html` now measures each coupling against the untouched
baseline of *its own* scene: five scenes × (one baseline + six couplings), all seeded, at
8 s. The table shows cold beads, active links, mean temperature, above-water beads and
frame p95; the log preserves each row and its difference from the scene baseline. This
uses the existing perf runner, not a second engine path. It is intentionally opt-in:
35 browser rows take several minutes and do not replace the ordinary cap matrix. The
8 s snapshot highlights scene-dependent effects but is not a long-term balance verdict.
