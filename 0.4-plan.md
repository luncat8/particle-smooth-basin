# 0.4 a scene you can tune, and a scene worth watching

## goal

Two things the 0.3 release left undone:

1. The default scene was wrong as a picture and as physics. At 0.3.3 it spawned 810 beads at 50% coverage with the waterline at 42% of the height; the pile reached two thirds of the way up the window, stood out of the water, and never reached the cold band. Instead of a basin, it was a full glass of beads.
2. Every number behind that scene lived in `js/engine.js`. Changing the thermal picture meant editing constants and reloading, and a scene could not be shared, compared or reported in a log.

0.4 makes the default scene a sparse, convecting bed, and turns every knob that shapes it into a control that can be copied out and pasted back.

## the shipped scene

| control | value | why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| canvas coverage | 21% | 340 beads at 1280×800 — a bed, not a filling |
| water level | 30% | the surface sits well above the bed; the top third of the window is water |
| cold zone depth | 56% | the band covers the settled bed's top and the raft floating on the surface |
| hot zone top | 78% | a 176 px heated band that touches the bed's floor |
| cold / hot power | 2.5× / 2.5× | bonds appear within 2 s (327 active links) and keep re-forming |
| heat lifts | 0.40 | hot beads are lighter, so the floor overturns |
| thermal size / Archimedes | -0.35 / -2.5 | hot shrinks; the smaller hot bead is the more buoyant one |
| bond strength / vs temperature | 0.72 / 2.0 | a firm cold raft that tears when stirred |

Seeded, 1280×800, 40 s (`node experiments/balance.js 40`): 340 beads, bed top 385 px against a 240 px waterline, 484 active links, 223 cold beads, top/bottom band temperature 0.15/0.95, 5.5 hot beads carried above the bed mid-line per second, 503 links half a second after a splash recovering to 480. Frame cost 0.34 ms.

Three fixes made that scene possible:

- **The spawn lattice was narrower than the beads.** `2.4 * rBar` columns can hold `2*1.05*rBar` beads but not `2*1.28*rBar` ones, so every bed started interpenetrating and the solver blew it 200–350 px upward in the first second. `spawnBed` now packs each row from the radii it actually places.
- **Heat has to change density.** Size and Archimedes coupling alone never convected anything: hot beads shrank, neighbours shifted, and net vertical heat flux stayed at noise for 20 s. The effective density is now `dens * (1 - 2*lift*(T - 0.5))`, which the **Heat lifts** control drives.
- **The hot band was too thin to touch the bed.** `hotStart` 0.84 left 76 px of heated floor under a 300 px bed; 0.78 doubles the rising-hot-bead count from 4.0 to 7.3 per second.

## tuning in the page

- Sliders: cold zone depth, hot zone top, cold power, hot power, heat lifts, bond strength, bond vs temperature, water level, coverage (8–60%), radius, mass, conduction, thermal size, Archimedes exponent, buoyancy. Theme toggle and the size/Archimedes presets are unchanged.
- **Copy settings** puts one JSON line on the clipboard (with an `execCommand` fallback when the clipboard API is unavailable, so `file://` still works). **Paste settings** takes it back through `window.prompt`, re-deals the bed, and re-syncs every slider from the world. **Defaults** restores the shipped table.
- The settings layer lives in `js/engine.js` as `SETTINGS`, `readSettings(world)` and `applySettings(world, settings)`, because the engine owns every value and the page must never re-derive one. `applySettings` clamps through the same setters the sliders use, keeps keys the paste does not name, and returns the names of keys it ignored — a hand-edited line cannot leave the world in a state the panel cannot describe.

## tests

- `experiments/settings.js` (new node check): read → apply → read is a fixed point, partial pastes only move what they name, unknown keys are reported, out-of-range pastes clamp, the fields a paste sets are visible in a fresh `readSettings`, and a pasted shipped scene stays finite for 10 s.
- `experiments/smoke.js`: boots from markup values that deliberately disagree with the engine, then asserts the page re-syncs every slider, that Copy reports the live scene, that Paste drives the sliders and the readouts, that a bad paste is reported and changes nothing, and that Defaults restores the shipped table.
- `tests.html`, `thermal-zone` check: 8 s at 1× on the shipped scene must produce ≥100 cold beads, ≥200 active bonds, a colder top than bottom, fewer than 5% of beads above the waterline, a bed top that stays at or under the surface, a bed top inside the cold band, and a floor inside the hot band.
- `tests.html` perf matrix: the shipped scene is now the first row of every run, quick and full, so `run_tests.py` always reports the real default next to the stress cases (12 rows full, 3 quick).
- `experiments/balance.js` (new tool): sweeps full settings objects and reports geometry (bed top, waterline, beads above it), link counts at 2/4/8 s and the end, cold beads, band temperatures, net heat flux, the share of hot beads moving up, travel distance, splash/stir recovery, cost per frame, and up/down crossings of the bed mid-line — the last one is what separated a still bed (0/s) from a circulating one (5–7/s). Argument: `node experiments/balance.js [seconds] [WxH]`.

## acceptance

- The page opens on a sparse bed under the water, with visible cold links and beads circulating at the floor.
- Copy/paste round-trips a scene exactly, and the node check proves it.
- `python3 run_tests.py` stays a single, argument-free entry point; the node half is now six checks; the browser half measures the shipped scene first.
- A capture from a real machine is still required for frame times and pixels — this sandbox has no browser.
